✦ Crafted with Botanical Care ✦

Wild leaven, pure local wheat, and patient hands.

At Crust & Crumb Sourdough, our baking aligns with local rhythms. We collaborate closely with local grain growers who treat the earth with gentleness. Every crackling crust and soft, custard-like crumb reflects real ingredients raised in living soil, then fermented slowly to unlock rich flavors and easier digestion.
