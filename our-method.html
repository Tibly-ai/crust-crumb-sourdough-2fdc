Honors of the Hearth

Nourished by time,
slowed by hand.

We believe bread is a living connection to the land. At Crust & Crumb Sourdough, we eschew commercial yeasts, additives, and accelerated timelines in favor of local heritage grains, pure water, sea salt, and wild leaven.

Our method honors the traditional craft of artisan baking, where patience yields a loaf that is rich in deep, complex tang, beautifully blistered, and remarkably gentle on the body.

Our Three Pillars

How We Bake

Every loaf takes a full thirty-six hours of careful guidance, monitoring temperature, wild hydration, and slow maturation.

01

The Grain & The Culture

Our starter, "Penelope," is fed daily with organic, stone-ground rye and heirloom wheat. We partner directly with regional grain growers who prioritize soil health, giving our flour a robust, nutrient-dense profile full of earthy, floral notes.

02

The Cold Fermentation

We stretch, fold, and coil the dough by hand over a span of several hours. Next, the loaves undergo a 36-hour cold proof. This long, chilled rest allows the wild yeast to digest gluten and starches, unlocking complex lactic and acetic acids.

03

Fire, Steam & Blister

Baked directly on stone hearths under heavy clouds of early steam, our loaves expand dramatically. This intense burst of heat gelatinizes the starch on the surface, creating our signature thick, blistered, caramelized crust and an airy, tender interior crumb.
